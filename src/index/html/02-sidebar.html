Video decoding
Native video player (beta)
Plays video with Android's own player for smoother playback and less battery. Filters, zoom, motion smoothing, upscaling and the Bluetooth delay all run natively on the GPU. The app reloads to switch.
Frame-drop protection
Reduces effects to keep video smooth. Auto: only when a video starts skipping frames. Light: always.
Audio lead (no frame drops)
Plays the sound ahead of the video instead of delaying the picture, so video never drops frames. The visualizer is silent on video while this is on.
Decode compensation (+300 ms)
Delays the picture by 300 ms to keep it in sync with the sound.
Lossless latency (no dropped frames)
Fixes videos losing frames while the latency delay is on.
Delayed picture sharpness
Picture sharpness while the delay is on. Low is lightest but soft; High is sharper and uses more memory.
Lighten UI while video plays
Freezes animated backgrounds while a video plays to prevent dropped frames. They return when paused.
