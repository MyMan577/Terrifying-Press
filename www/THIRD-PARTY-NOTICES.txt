AI upscalers: QuickSRNet (small, medium), XLSR, SESR-M5
Trained weights from the Qualcomm AIMET Model Zoo (https://github.com/quic/aimet-model-zoo),
converted to GPU shaders by Press Play (tools/build_sr_models.py).
SESR design: "Collapsible Linear Blocks for Super-Efficient Super Resolution", ARM Ltd.
XLSR design: Ayazoglu, "Extremely Lightweight Quantization Robust Real-Time Single-Image Super Resolution".
QuickSRNet design: Berger et al., Qualcomm AI Research.
